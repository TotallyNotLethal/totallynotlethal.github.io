Spells meet in four ways. Each one is covered below.

  1. On a player. A hit leaves that element's mark for about 4.5 s. A different element landing on a marked player sets off that pair's reaction.
  2. In the air. Two projectiles that collide resolve through the same pair reaction.
  3. On the ground. A spell landing on a puddle, flames, ice, gas or acid reacts with it. Every pair has at least a generic reaction.
  4. On a spell object. Walls, waves, cyclones and wells each have their own reactions.

Mark rules

  • Damage becomes hit × multiplier + bonus, and a burst splashes around the target.
  • One hit can set off up to 3 reactions if the target carries several marks. The strongest goes first.
  • Reactions use up the old mark. Only Burning and Chilled stack, up to 5 each.
  • Six pairs cancel instead of amplify, shown in teal on the grid. Five of them also stop the new mark from landing. Renewal lets it land.
